About Us

Our History

Founded in the heart of Ikom, Cross River State, HillSide High School has a rich tradition of academic excellence and moral discipline. We have grown into a leading institution dedicated to producing well-rounded individuals ready to face global challenges.

Our Mission

To provide a conducive learning environment that fosters academic brilliance, moral integrity, and innovative thinking, equipping students with the skills needed to excel in their chosen paths.

Our Vision

To be the premier secondary educational institution in Nigeria, recognized for cultivating leadership, character, and lifelong learning in every student.

Core Values

  • Integrity
  • Discipline
  • Innovation
